# The Four Pillars

This portfolio is organised around four capabilities every data-driven consultant needs.

## 1️⃣ Data & SQL — Asking the Right Questions

Businesses sit on mountains of data. SQL is how you interrogate it.

**Focus:** writing queries that answer commercial questions — sales trends, customer behaviour, performance gaps — clearly and efficiently.

**Case studies:**
- Retail Sales Performance Review — *Which products, regions and periods drive revenue, and where are we underperforming?*
- Customer Purchase Patterns — *Who are our most valuable customers, and what do they buy together?*
- HR Attrition Analysis — *Which departments lose people fastest, and what do leavers have in common?*

📁 `sql/`

---

## 2️⃣ Python — Analysis & Automation

Python for business analysis: cleaning messy data, finding patterns, and automating repetitive reporting work.

**Focus:** Pandas, data cleaning, exploratory analysis, and clear written findings — the kind of analysis that goes into a client report.

**Case studies:**
- Sales Data Deep-Dive — *What does a year of transactions reveal that a monthly report misses?*
- Automated Reporting — *How much analyst time can we save by automating a weekly report?*
- Market Basket Analysis — *Which product bundles should we promote?*

📁 `python/`

---

## 3️⃣ Business Intelligence — Telling the Story

Insight only matters if decision-makers can see it. Power BI and Tableau turn analysis into dashboards executives actually use.

**Focus:** dashboard design, KPI selection, and data storytelling — building views for a CEO, a sales director, and an operations manager from the same data.

**Power BI case studies:**
- Executive Sales Dashboard — *One page, five KPIs, zero confusion.*
- Finance Performance Dashboard — *Budget vs actuals at a glance.*
- HR Insights Dashboard — *Headcount, attrition and hiring trends for people leaders.*

**Tableau case studies:**
- Customer Analytics Story — *A guided walkthrough of who our customers are.*
- Marketing Campaign Dashboard — *Which channels earn their budget?*
- Regional Sales Map — *Geography as a lens on performance.*

**Digital analytics (Google Analytics 4):**
- Website Performance Review — *Where does our traffic come from, and where does the purchase funnel leak?* (GA4 demo account, Google Merchandise Store)

📁 `powerbi/` · `tableau/`

---

## 4️⃣ AI in Business — Value, Not Hype

AI matters to businesses when it changes a decision, saves money, or opens a new opportunity. This pillar focuses on applied AI: where it fits, what it's worth, and how to adopt it responsibly.

**Focus:** practical AI use cases, business impact, and plain-English explanations of how it works.

**Case studies:**
- Customer Churn Prediction — *Which customers are about to leave, and what is keeping them worth?*
- Customer Segmentation — *How should we group customers to personalise offers?*
- AI Use-Case Briefs — *Short, non-technical write-ups: where AI creates value in retail, finance, HR and healthcare.*

📁 `ai-in-business/`
